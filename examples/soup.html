A primordial soup of Z80 machine code

4,096 random 16-byte programs sit on a 64 × 64 grid. In each update about 1,600 neighbouring pairs meet: the two tapes are joined into one 32-byte ring and executed for 128 steps on an emulated Z80, then split back. There is no goal and no fitness function, only random meetings and a sprinkling of mutations. Watch for a program that copies itself to appear and sweep the grid. 

Starting…

Colours identify tapes: one colour spreading means one program is copying itself. The self-copy test runs the most common tape against 16 random partners and counts how often the partner half ends up as a copy of it (at least 12 of 16 bytes, best cyclic alignment).
