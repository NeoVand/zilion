# 6502 decimal mode against Clark's NMOS algorithm

Generated by `python -I tools/zt.py decimal6502 <6502 vectors>` (module `tools/ztest/decimal6502.py`, whose docstring has the transcription and how two open points of the document are read). A gate of `npm run test:harness`.

Reference: Bruce Clark, "Decimal Mode", 6502.org Tutorials, Appendix A (http://www.6502.org/tutorials/decimal_mode.html). 6502 (NMOS) table: ADC accumulator and C from Seq. 1, N and V from Seq. 2, Z binary; SBC accumulator from Seq. 3, C, N, V and Z binary.

Open points of the document and their readings: step 2c ("signed (twos complement) arithmetic") does not say the width; A & $F0 and B & $F0 are read as signed bytes. With that reading step 1 agrees with every decimal-mode test; read unsigned it would agree with 107,375 of 154,975. Step 3b's `& $0F` of a negative number is the value mod 16 under any reading. ARR's decimal mode (undocumented) is not in the document and is checked by the vectors only.

Result: **passed**. Core SHA-256 `4f4baf4dad24e724d978045be31f17ade978a56a95f77ca43e7186a0e2a765ad`.
